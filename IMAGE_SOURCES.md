## Notes

- McDonald's images are sourced from McDonald's UAE product pages or UAE delivery-menu image sources. Meal-size variants may share the same matching meal image where a size-specific photo is not published.
- KFC images are sourced from KFC UAE or Talabat UAE menu item photos.
- Pizza Hut images are sourced from Talabat UAE menu item photos.
- Papa Johns images are sourced from the official Papa Johns UAE menu's linked product images.
- Dunkin' images are sourced from Dunkin UAE menu imagery or UAE delivery-menu product imagery. The Choco Frosted Donut photo is a Dunkin regional menu image.
- Starbucks images are sourced from Starbucks UAE's featured menu at its Khalifa University, Abu Dhabi location page. This confirms product imagery and names, not MBZ branch availability or price.
- An image source may be a UAE menu or brand page rather than the precise MBZ outlet. The `imageSource` field records the actual source context.
